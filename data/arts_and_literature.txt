1. WHAT ARE ARTS AND LITERATURE?

Literature is writing that is valued for its beauty, imagination, or ideas. It includes novels, poems, plays, short stories, and essays. Art in a broad sense includes painting, sculpture, drawing, architecture, music, and performance. Together they express human feelings, stories, and ways of seeing the world.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. FAMOUS BOOKS AND STORIES

Some books have been loved by readers for generations. Fairy tales and folk stories such as those collected by the Brothers Grimm or written by Hans Christian Andersen teach lessons through simple characters and clear plots.

Classic children’s and young adult books include Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland by Lewis Carroll, which follows a girl through a strange and dreamlike world. Treasure Island by Robert Louis Stevenson is an adventure story about pirates and buried treasure. The Chronicles of Narnia by C.S. Lewis take readers into a magical land. Charlotte’s Web by E.B. White is a gentle story about friendship between a pig and a spider.

In modern times, the Harry Potter series by J.K. Rowling became one of the most popular book series in the world. It follows a young wizard and his friends as they face challenges at a school of magic. The stories mix adventure, friendship, courage, and the struggle between good and evil. J.K. Rowling created a detailed magical world with its own rules, creatures, and history, and the books encouraged millions of young people to enjoy reading.

Other well-known works include To Kill a Mockingbird by Harper Lee, which deals with justice and prejudice, and The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings by J.R.R. Tolkien, which create rich fantasy worlds filled with hobbits, elves, dwarves, and epic quests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. IMPORTANT AUTHORS

William Shakespeare lived in England around four hundred years ago and wrote plays and poems that are still performed and studied. His works include Romeo and Juliet, Hamlet, and Macbeth. He had a deep understanding of human emotions and used language in powerful ways.

Jane Austen wrote novels about life, manners, and relationships in early nineteenth-century England. Her books include Pride and Prejudice and Sense and Sensibility.

Charles Dickens wrote about life in Victorian England and often showed the struggles of poor people. Famous works include Oliver Twist and A Christmas Carol.

Mark Twain, an American author, wrote The Adventures of Tom Sawyer and Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, stories that capture life along the Mississippi River with humor and honesty.

J.K. Rowling is a modern British author best known for the Harry Potter series. She showed that stories written for young readers can also appeal to adults and can create a worldwide shared culture of reading.

Many other authors from different countries and times have shaped literature. Reading their works helps students understand different lives, times, and ideas.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. CLASSIC ART

Art has existed since prehistoric times when people painted animals on cave walls. Ancient Egyptians created wall paintings and sculptures for tombs. The Greeks and Romans made statues that showed the human body with great skill.

During the Renaissance in Europe, artists such as Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, and Raphael created masterpieces. Leonardo painted the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. Michelangelo sculpted David and painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel. These works showed a new interest in realism, perspective, and human emotion.

Later artists explored different styles. Impressionist painters such as Claude Monet tried to capture light and momentary impressions. Vincent van Gogh used bold colors and expressive brushstrokes in works such as The Starry Night. Pablo Picasso helped develop Cubism, which showed objects from multiple viewpoints at once.

Sculpture, architecture, and other visual arts also form part of classic art. Famous buildings such as the Parthenon in Greece, Gothic cathedrals in Europe, and modern designs continue to inspire people.

Books and art help us imagine lives different from our own. They teach empathy, creativity, and careful observation. They preserve culture and history. Reading widely and looking at art carefully improve language skills, critical thinking, and the ability to express ideas.
